# Statement grading rubric (T-242, from 25 Nov 2026)
Each pack row carries question_context (model-written topic of the analyst's question).
- R: verbatim on the page; management speaker who said it (last "Name:" label, this or previous page, hand-overs); full name; substantive; sensible topic.
  The referent counts as present when question_context holds it AND question_context matches, at least in substance, the analyst question printed before the answer.
- P: speaker first-name/title/empty; referent missing from both statement and question_context; mid-sentence start; topic tag wrong.
- W: not verbatim; analyst/moderator/wrong manager as speaker.
- J: greeting, thanks, hand-over, logistics, cover letter, disclaimer, duplicate.
Extra column per row: qc = match | mismatch | empty (does question_context match the printed analyst question?). Report every mismatch: a grade borrowed from a mismatched question_context is not R.
