**Status:** proposed research plan, not authority to run it. **Clock:** at most 100 person-hours; stop early when a gate fails. **Capital:** parked; paper research only. **Source:** [`Reality Engine — Exhaustive Specshe.txt`](./Reality%20Engine%20%E2%80%94%20Exhaustive%20Specshe.txt), especially §§2–6 and its 2026-09-28 addendum. ## Decision and scope

**Question:** Do *explicit raises in management guidance relative to a prior comparable concall* contain information associated with subsequent positive returns, after the transcript's **first public release**, beyond contemporaneous comparable stocks and observable price reaction? Test one long-only, 90-calendar-day hypothesis if existing data permit. This is a test of conditional drift, **not** proof that delayed interpretation caused it; underlying earnings news is a competing explanation.

The initial 100 hours **do not** drain document queues, collect new feeds, fix the live DB, upgrade RAM, restart embedding services, create a new ensemble, implement shorts, or deploy capital. Current price-only momentum/reversal and killed F1/F2 are **not** strategy candidates here; pre-event price returns may be *matching variables* only. An existing F1/F2 KILL or REVOKED verdict remains unchanged. This is not a promise of an alpha-positive result. Correct outcome may be **NOT READY** by hour 34.

### Frozen hypothesis card: decisions to fill from evidence by hour 42

- **Population/frame:** enumerate all documented first-publication concall events in a *date-eligible, auditable* liquidity universe; predeclare whether the test labels the entire eligible frame or a randomly selected capped subset. Estimate and describe **only the selected frame's supported population**; never present a convenience sample as all concalls. Do not treat today's N200 survivors as historical members. A ten-stock N200 list in the spec is a **smoke/acceptance sample**, not a powered discovery universe (§4). Report processed and pending distribution to bound generalizability.
- **Label:** `RAISE` only for an explicit, attributable management guidance revision against a comparable *previously public* call; record ISIN, symbol, fiscal period/confidence ≥0.5, real event date, source document/version, public-release timestamp and quoted chunk span. Unchanged, incomparable, missing or ambiguous text is **NULL/not labeled**. No numeric ₹cr/% inferred from prose. Auditors sample both positives and apparently negative candidates to assess false positives **and false negatives**; set agreement and minimum precision/recall requirements in the signed card.
- **Clock:** prefer exchange first-publication timestamp in IST. The **eligibility of date-only publication records** must be decided before outcomes; if accepted, report them as a distinct precision tier and enter at the close of the *next full trading session* after that dated release. Timestamped records also enter at the close of the first full session after confirmed release. No call date, system ingest date from historical backfill, later revised transcript or arbitrary lag masquerades as first publication. Choose the first tradable close on or after entry + 90 calendar days for exit and use the same convention in controls. A stricter additional processing delay can be frozen. Count right-censored events explicitly.
- **Statistic:** one-sided *long-only* mean 90-calendar-day **simple** return excess of RAISE events versus same-entry/exit-window eligible peer basket. Equal weight within event-date/season cohorts and then across cohorts, with this weighting written in the card; report the number of independent seasons/firms, not just chunks. No SELL, stop-as-gain or implied short return. Pre-event matching may use trailing return, volatility and traded value if present as-of entry; use sector only when dated coverage is verified. Report index-relative return as descriptive only when its composition/series is valid. Price-reaction matching tests whether guidance adds information beyond the initial earnings reaction.
- **Statistical gate:** prespecify uncertainty accounting for correlated firms and overlapping time intervals (e.g. calendar/season and firm clusters, with a calendar-time check). If too few independent clusters, declare **INCONCLUSIVE–POWER**, regardless of naive event-count significance. Check whether the *original* promotion requirements (≥30 weekly cohorts, clustered hit-rate lower bound >0.5, same sign across two defined regimes, shuffle placebo |mean| ≤100 bps, ≥500 bps net **measured** costs) can even be evaluated; **never relax** them to call this promoted. Historical price data have already been examined by prior F2 work, so a time split is a **descriptive stability check, not a genuinely sealed holdout**.
- **Failure control:** prespecify one within-firm, comparable-season shuffle-entry placebo and exact sample/aggregation conventions; an effect beyond the spec's ±100 bps bound voids interpretation. Review known SWANDEF-type unadjusted jumps and the REVOKED SELL sign artifact as regression fixtures. Multiple candidate thresholds, universes, horizons or labels tested afterward are exploratory and must be listed in the attempt ledger.
- **Economics:** assume **no shortability** without instrument/borrow data. Report **RAISE long-only selection only**; this test neither labels CUTs nor evaluates avoid/underweight decisions. Show gross excess; net of **stated assumed transaction-cost ranges**, before tax; estimate capacity as order size versus *pre-event* traded value at a fixed predeclared participation bound. Do not claim measured slippage, executable ₹10cr returns, precise tax or the SIP's 7.06% XIRR as a directly comparable benchmark. The spec's ₹10cr mandate, ≤40% cash/gold/silver allowance, taxes and market impact require a later portfolio-level study (§3, §6).

**Potential result threshold, to freeze after return-blind power review:** a historical lead requires positive direction and an economically relevant effect (the spec uses +500 bps net *measured* as its stricter promotion bar), an uncertainty interval excluding zero under prespecified dependence handling, no disqualifying placebo or source error, adequate label quality, and same predicted sign in two **predefined, adequately represented** regimes. If regime definition/sample is unavailable, it is **INCONCLUSIVE**, not a lead. A statistically imprecise estimate is INCONCLUSIVE; FAIL requires adequate power to exclude the prespecified worthwhile effect or decisive wrong-sign evidence. Actual numerical research-screen threshold, cost assumption and alpha/multiplicity rule belong in the frozen card, **not after inspecting returns**. Primary matched-peer excess can support only this *research lead*; the spec's separate **universe-index-relative** bar remains untested unless valid historical index series and membership also exist. A lead is never an assertion of promoted net alpha.

- Anchor OMP to the **desktop** live checkout and interpreter from scheduled-task actions; this workspace contains only the specsheet. Recheck task states, lock/WAL and DB timestamps. One auditable query at a time, time/memory bounded by *actual* observations. No claim that 3400 MT/s or RAM saturation dictates throughput.

Avoid writing numeric guidance derived from prose. Treat report outputs as untrusted until independently replayed.

## Verdict and handoff

| Label | Required finding | Subsequent action |
|---|---|---|
| **NOT READY—DATA / AUTHORITY** | Missing authority, release/version provenance, clean prices, trusted labels, eligible control or defensible sample. | Report exact blocking field, affected numerator/denominator, and smallest future data repair. No alpha inference. |
| **INCONCLUSIVE—POWER / METHOD** | Valid data but insufficient independent firms/seasons, uncertain estimated effect, regime/control ambiguity or missing sign-off. | Report precision limit; no threshold changes or post-hoc variant sweep. |
| **VOID—METHOD** | Look-ahead, silent sample selection, contaminated price, wrong sign, failed predeclared placebo or changed card. | Record incident; discard that run's positive/negative inference. |
| **FAIL—HYPOTHESIS** | Valid, adequately powered test decisively rejects the fixed worthwhile effect or has convincing opposite direction. | Record failed attempt; do not retune F1/F2 or this hypothesis on the same results. |
| **HISTORICAL RESEARCH LEAD** | One frozen test meets its separate research-screen criteria, independent review and adverse checks. | Paper-only candidate **subject to** forward cohorts, PIT-universe validation and measured cost/tax/capacity work. `recommend_apply:false`. |

**Hard ceiling:** 100 person-hours cannot manufacture independent forward cohorts, measured slippage, clean historical membership or missing first releases. The existing promotion bar in the spec (§3) stays binding; testing one 90-day horizon against matched peers does **not** satisfy its entire horizon/universe/index benchmark grid. The report must distinguish a *stock-selection research lead* from a deployable ₹10cr net-of-tax trading edge.
