IMPORTANT OPERATIONAL 
EVENTS FOR Q3 FY 2024

=== PAGE 5 ===
Highlights
-
Delivered two Electric Hybrid 100 Pax Water Metro boat to Kochi Metro.
-
Delivered two Hybrid Electric Catamaran Passenger vessels to IWAI.
-
Contract was signed with Ministry of Defence for a value of Rs.488.25
crores for repair and maintenance of the equipments and systems onboard
INS Vikrant.
-
Ministry of Defence, inked a deal with CSL for the Mid Life Upgrade and Re-
Powering of 'INS Beas' at a cost of Rs. 313.42 Crs.
-
Launched three nos. of Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Crafts
(ASWSWC) being built for Indian Navy.

=== PAGE 6 ===
Highlights 
-
CSL awarded seed funding of Rs. 30 Lakhs each to 3 start-ups through IIMK
(LIVE) backed USHUS Marine Start Up Support Program.
-
After completion of all the civil works, ISRF and the New Dry-dock was
inaugurated by the Hon’ble Prime Minister on January 17, 2024.
-
Keel laid 2 nos. of Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Crafts (ASWSWC)
being built for Indian Navy.
-
Steel cutting of Trailer Suction Hopper Dredger being built for Dredging
Corporation of India Limited (DCI) was held at CSL.

=== PAGE 7 ===
Highlights
-
CSL was honoured with Maritime Excellence Award during INMEX SMM
India Summit
in recognition for its commitment towards sustainable
solutions and the stellar performance in shipbuilding, under the category,
‘Shipbuilding Company of the Year’.
-
CSL was honoured with the Maritime Excellence Achievers’ Award for being
the Best in the Industry under the category ‘Ship Building and Repair’ during
the Global Maritime India Summit (GMIS) 2023.
-
CSL upgraded to “GreenCo Gold” ratings from “GreenCo Silver ratings by
the Confederation of Indian Industry.

=== PAGE 8 ===
Highlights 
-
Udupi Cochin Shipyard Limited (UCSL) Keel Laid the second 70T Bollard
Pull Tug being built for Polestar Maritime Limited.
-
UCSL delivered one no. of 62 Tonne Bollard Pull Tug, being built for Ocean
Sparkle Limited, the leading tug operator in India.
-
Steel cutting of 2 nos. of 3800 T Dry cargo vessel being built for M/s. Wilson
Shipowning AS, Norway was held at UCSL.
