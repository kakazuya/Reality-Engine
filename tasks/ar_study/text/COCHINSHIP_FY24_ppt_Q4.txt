=== PAGE 5 ===
Highlights – Orders
-
January, 2024 – Contract signed with a European Client for the design and
construction of a Hybrid Service Operation Vessel (Hybrid SOV). The vessel is to be
delivered in 2026.
-
March 2024 – Hooghly Cochin Shipyard Limited (HCSL) secured an order for
construction of 2 nos. of 40T Bollard Pull ASD Tugs for Industrial Handling Pvt. Ltd.
-
April 2024 – Udupi Cochin Shipyard Limited (UCSL) secured an order for 1 no. of
70T Bollard Pull Tug for Polestar Maritime Limited.
-
May 2024 – CSL also bagged another order from the same European Client for the
design and construction of a Hybrid SOV with an option for two more such vessels.
The project is expected to be completed by the end 2026.

=== PAGE 6 ===
Highlights – Orders
-
May 2024 – UCSL bagged an order for construction of 3 nos. of 70T Bollard
Pull Tugs for Ocean Sparkle Limited.
-
February 2024 – CSL entered into a contract with the Indian Navy for
undertaking Medium Refits of Two Indian Naval Vessels.
-
April 2024 – CSL signed the Master Ship Repair Agreement (MSRA) with
United States Navy. This will facilitate repair of US Naval vessels under Military
Sealift Command in CSL.

=== PAGE 8 ===
Highlights – Major Events
-
January 2024 – Hon’ble Prime Minister dedicated the International Ship
Repair Facility (ISRF) and the world’s first stepped Dry-dock to the nation,
marking a significant milestone in the augmentation of India’s Maritime
capability.
-
February 2024 – Steel cutting completed for a Zero Emission Feeder
Container vessel being built for a European client.
-
February 2024 – Keel laying of three nos. of Hybrid Electric Catamaran
Passenger vessels for Inland Waterways Authority of India (IWAI) was held
HCSL.

carried out successfully at CSL, International Ship Repair Facility (ISRF)
Jetty, Kochi.
