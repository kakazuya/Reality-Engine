No-ticket: ticket system rework asked for by Shivam 10 Nov (titles alone do not say why a ticket was opened or what closes it; creation is ad hoc)
Goal: make every ticket self-explaining and creation robust. Each ticket carries: title, why (the failure or request that opened it, with date and source), close_when (a checkable condition), accept (a runnable command, or "Shivam" for a human sign-off), owner, links (briefs, reports, commits), and a history of status changes with reasons. The Excel/HTML board shows Why and Close when as their own columns.
Evidence: today the board is one pipe-separated line per ticket in tasks/TASKS.md (`- T-NNN | status | P | title | accept: ... | from: ... | opened <date>`), written by reality_engine/scripts/task_board.py (add/set/done/drop/list/card/export/lint) and rendered by reality_engine/scripts/ticket_board_report.py to D:/RealityEngine/reports/ticket_board.xlsx and .html (groups Open, In progress, Pending a bottleneck, Closed, Sidelined). Problems seen 10 Nov: `done` executes the accept text, so prose accepts ("reports/ list of 10 ...") fail and tickets get closed with `drop` instead; rows owned by a closed session stay "DOING Wolf" for days; no field says why a row exists; merged work stays OPEN until someone remembers; standing rules were filed as tickets; `export` without the report script writes a poorer sheet. A post-commit hook and the session hook read this board (grep for task_board and ticket_board_report in reality_engine/scripts and .git/hooks before changing formats).
Steps:
1. Storage: keep tasks/TASKS.md human-readable but give each ticket a block (title line plus indented `why:`, `close_when:`, `accept:`, `links:`, `history:` lines), or move the data to tasks/tickets.jsonl and generate TASKS.md from it; choose the one that keeps the existing commands, hooks and tests working, and say why.
2. `add` requires --why (>= 30 characters) and --close-when; --accept must be either a command that the board can run (validated: the named test file exists) or `Shivam: <what he approves>`; reject a title that states a rule ("always", "never", "every worker must") with a pointer to `ruling add`.
3. `done` runs a command accept or requires --shivam for a Shivam accept and records who/when/commit; add `close --merged <commit>` that records the merge commit; `set` records every change in history with a reason.
4. `lint` flags: DOING owned by a session not seen for 24 h, OPEN with no activity for 7 days, accept not runnable, missing why/close_when, a title that reads as a rule.
5. ticket_board_report: add columns Why, Close when, Owner, Last change, Links to both the xlsx and html; keep the existing groups.
6. Migration: existing rows get why/close_when empty and are flagged by lint (a separate triage fills them; do not invent text).
