## 5. Rules applied while building it

- Every trigger is sourced: a `policy_events` row (`quote_ok=1`), a company disclosure, or a market series. Rows without a `doc_id` in `macro_events` are never used.
- `exists_today` is not an opinion: each hop names a real table and the builder fails the run if a hop claims `yes`/`partial` and names no table that exists in `sqlite_master`.
- Every `no` or `partial` hop carries a schema proposal; the builder refuses a row that does not.
- Coverage is the weakest hop. A chain with 3 answerable hops and 1 missing one is `partial`, and the missing hop is named.
- Lags are ours or labelled an assumption; no elasticity is invented.
