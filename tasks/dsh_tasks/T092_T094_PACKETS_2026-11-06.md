## T-092 Complete guidance

**Scouted facts (6 Nov).** `guidance.py` is 1,059 lines and works metric first: a sentence survives only if it matches the fixed `METRICS` list (lines 154-182), passes `plausible` ranges and a chain of filters (lines 831-947).

- `numbers()` returns nothing for counts, tonnes or multiples: "2,100 stores", "110 million to 130 million tons", "2x", "400,000 units". `_explicit_period` returns nothing for "by Dec 2025", "by Mar 2027", "by 2030".
- Earlier rounds added words company by company (`NOV`, `IRP`, `APE`). That cannot reach the universe, where subjects are unbounded: dark stores, cement capacity, charging points, launches, R&D spend.

**Chosen method: subject first, capture before classify.** Capture any management sentence with a forward cue and a stated quantity, keep the subject as words from the sentence, and treat the canonical `metric` as an optional label, `other` when unknown. Precision comes from what a sentence is (question, past fact, someone else's forecast), not from a list of allowed metrics.

**Fallback.** If subject phrases are too noisy on the dev set, keep the verbatim clause around the number as `subject` and rely on `pilot3_search`-style matching over it. Do not go back to a fixed metric list.
