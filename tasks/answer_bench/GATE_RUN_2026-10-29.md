## 5. Method / deviations

- The hand-60 rerun is the *current* code on the *current* stores, not the stored pilot answers: two rows (MB0010#L, MB0122#L) now carry the asked public-holding row the pilot answer lacked, and the gate still downgrades them because the answer mixes in rows outside the named month.
- The gate judges only the row fields the answer shows: a hidden numeric key ("high": 1710.0) is not evidence that the answer reported a high, and a fallback ask needs two key terms, not one generic word. Event/relation asks got their own aliases (control change, auditor resignation, chart pattern, NIM, loan growth, lag, credit cost, options).
- The strict grader recomputes the gate from the 8 recorded rows and the hop's rendered detail, never from the planner's flags. It disagrees with the planner's flags on 1,591 rows (2,817 hop flags): the record caps rows at 8 and detail at 600 characters, so a concept carried only by a later row is invisible to it. The grader's verdict is the one reported; the disagreement is counted, not resolved in the planner's favour.
- The bank was re-run end to end under the fixed gate (1,073 originals + 1,608 instances); the earlier partial run's files are kept in .tmp/ as *_v1.jsonl.
- Pilot stores were read immutable: REALITY_CHUNKS_V3=D:\RealityEngine\pilot\pilot60\chunks_v3_pilot.db, REALITY_PILOT_T1=\pilot\pilot60, REALITY_MACRO_PILOT_DB=\pilot\macro_all.db.
- 2026-10-29 hardening (this run). The 10-row hand check of the previous strict ANSWERED set found 4 OK / 3 PARTIAL / 3 WRONG, so the ask gate now requires every named part: promoter AND public holding, share count AND promoter holding, business AND segments, raw AND adjusted, the exchange when the question asks for it ("on which exchange?", "nse bse") and the side of the RSI band (an "oversold days" row does not answer "RSI stayed above 70"). A judgement ask ("has the debt fallen, or has it moved into CWIP and JVs") needs a row carrying that metric: a price return is not it.
- The unrelated-row penalty is judged per hop *and* per numeric claim the answer prints: two unrelated rows, or one that carries a number, make the case PARTIAL, and `unrelated_rows` is reported per row and in the bank totals.
- Instance questions are regenerated with the instance company's own name (`instantiate.py --rename-only`: 1,408 questions renamed, deterministic, 0 left naming the parent). The approved bank is untouched; its defects are in `tasks/master_bank/BANK_DEFECTS_2026-10-29.md`.
- Before/after on the same 2,681 result rows: strict ANSWERED was 137 (5.1%) on main 3489a87; this hardening leaves 24 (0.9%), and 19 of the 20 hand-checked rows of that remainder are right (the 20th is an under-specified industry ask, a bank defect). Recall is the cost of the rule: the answerer still cannot print the exchange, the adjusted basis, an episode list or page content, so those asks are now PARTIAL until the planners render them.
