# Gate 95% review, 31 Oct 2026

Independent read-only review. Run data: the v4 bank run in worktree `20261030T231148Z-597b2681`, read at about 05:38 IST. My snapshot had 224 contract-answered rows; the worker's final strict count is 221. The gap does not change any conclusion.

## Verdict

- **95% truly correct is not reachable on the bank as it stands.** About 57-62% of rows cannot be truly answered as posed: 34.6% name a company outside the pilot (measured), and another 23-28% are instances whose premise is false for the substituted company (estimated).
- **Today:** about 0.6% truly correct (3 of 40 hand-checked; range 0.2-1.6%). The 8.2% is a contract count.
- **With the fixes below:** my estimate is 8-15% after about 15-20 worker-hours and 30-55% after about 40-60. These ranges are low confidence. I cannot see 95% from here without Shivam's scope ruling and a working document reader.
- **Biggest lever:** fix what is being measured, then the bank. Grade against the bank's own rubric, which no grader reads, and repair the instances. Everything else can only be steered once those two are done.

#### 1. Grade truth, not contract

**Do:** add a correctness grade beside the contract count.
- Score each answer against the bank's `rubric.required_steps` (743 of 1,073 parents), `forbidden` (743) and `ground_truth` (203).
- Hold a fixed stratified panel of 150 rows, hand-graded, plus a fresh random 40 each run, so the panel cannot be overfitted.
- Let a free-model judge do the bulk work only after it agrees with the hand panel at 90% or better.
- This only tightens the grader. It loosens nothing.

**Evidence:**
- `grade_v2.py:192-226` takes its parts from the decomposer's own Ask, so the system marks its own homework. A decomposer that emits fewer parts scores higher. Single-part cases pass at 19% (92/482), and cases with 3 parts at 7% (27/388), both measured.
- A grep for `required_steps`, `ground_truth`, `forbidden` and `required_evidence` finds no match in `grade_v1.py`, `grade_v2.py`, `gate_run.py` or `query/v4/`. Most of the bank's gold has never been used.
- The old hardened grader had 19/20 precision (decision log, 29 Oct 11:25). The v4 grader has 3/40. The instrument got worse.
- 73 of 101 contract-answered rows with a gold parent contain every gold-required measure (measured), yet hand precision is 7.5%. So the 77.6% and 84.8% decomposer scores measure concept names, not operators or windows. They cannot see the failure that matters.

**Risk:** a free-model judge can be lenient, so the hand panel stays the authority.

**Cheap test:** apply the rubric by hand to the 40 sampled rows. If its verdicts agree with the hand check on fewer than 30 rows, the rubric is not usable and only the panel remains.

#### 3. Repair instances, add `premise_false` (needs Shivam's approval)

**Do:** this is a declared correction with evidence.
- Rule-check every instance's premise against the stores.
- Assign templates only to names where the premise holds (same sector, same business type), or mark that parent "not instantiable" with a reason.
- Add a typed `premise_false` answer that quotes the evidence ("HEGAM did not fall 20.15% on 14 May; it moved X%"), counted in its own column and never added to `real_answered` without his ruling.

**Evidence:**
- 34 of 38 checkable "rose/fell N% on date" premises in instance text are false against the stored close (measured; 57 rows carry such a premise, 19 could not be checked).
- A hand read of 50 random stock instances gives 20 clearly false or naming another company, and 4 more doubtful: 40-48% (95% range about 28-54%). Examples: MB0534#L "BAJFINANCE's order book is Rs 9,923 crore", MB0533#L "HINDUNILVR's order inflow", MB0485#MI an airline question for PANACHE, MB1039#MS "Tata Power's RSI" for NLCINDIA, MB0919#MI tobacco duty for Bigbloc.
- Nothing can correctly answer these under the current rule, because a true "the premise is wrong" counts as not answered (`grade_v2.py` part_status).

**Risk:** this could look like shrinking the denominator to raise the score. Mitigation: keep the old count visible, list every row changed, and let Shivam approve each class. Choosing only easy names would also bias the bucket mix, so keep large, mid-small and micro balanced.

**Cheap test:** have Shivam read 30 of my flagged rows. If he calls fewer than 20 of them ill-posed, drop the idea.

#### 4. Evidence reader over documents

**Do:** for passage and "what did they say" parts:
1. Filter chunks by entity, document type and `known_at <= as_of` before any model sees them.
2. Retrieve with FTS plus local bge-small (no paid embeddings).
3. Have free Beam return an answer and a quote, or "not stated".
4. Reject any answer whose quote is not verbatim in the retrieved chunk. The Beam pre-read already has a `quote_ok` field.

Shivam needs to rule on this. The design says the LLM only writes the connecting prose and "never sets a number" (`QUERY_V4_DESIGN.md:1116-1120`). Quoting a verified passage with no model-set number fits that, but it does extend the model's role. Numbers would still come from a parser or the quote itself.

**Evidence:**
- Text parts: 97 of 1,116 pass even the contract (measured). The claim table is concalls only (647,516 rows, no decks or annual reports) with 46% in topic `other` and 127 topics, while asks use free words such as "commissioned", "tender" and "mou".
- `chunks_v3_pilot.db` holds 108,078 chunks across five document types, and the executor does not read them.
- 5 of the 6 management-quote golds I tested on the row's own pilot entity reached at least 80% word match in that entity's chunks (MB0337, MB0345, MB0355, MB0415, MB0540). MB0517 did not, and its quote may belong to another company in its peer list. Golds that are computed values were not a useful test.
- 12 failed questions with hand-picked search terms: about 5 had clearly on-topic text from snippets, 2-3 partial, 4 none. Naive OR search on question words mostly returns letterheads and boilerplate, so retrieval needs real work.

**Against it:** Beam found 0 direct and 8 partial on the 74 proxy tickets (`nid_beam_preread.json`), and left 74% of label work as `other`. Those are the hardest cases with blunt search logs, so that does not settle it, but it is a warning.

**Risk:** a wrong reading passes with a real quote. Answers would need a hand audit each run. It also adds query-time model dependence, and the free tier may not hold at scale.

**Cheap test:** 40 well-posed passage questions, free Beam, about 1 worker-hour, hand-graded. Under 50% right: kill it and fall back to better retrieval with quotes only.

**Risk:** the contract number will fall when this lands. That is correct, and Shivam should be told in advance. Forward studies need care around look-ahead.

**Cheap test:** build one operator (near-high count and forward return) for MB1044 and MB1047 across all instances, then hand-grade them.

#### 6. Scope ruling and set answers (Shivam decides)

**Do:** he has said "no re-scope" (`DECISION_LOG.md:329`), so these are options, not a recommendation to shrink. The deadlock: chunking the full universe is held until 95%, while 34.6% of rows need names that only full data would serve.
- **529 parents with instances:** (a) count each through its instances and report the parents apart, or (b) widen the data pilot to the named companies. There are about 394 distinct names, and structured data is already held for them. The old engine answered 25 of the lost regress rows that way.
- **371 parents without instances:** mostly industry, theme and market-wide. `run.py:216-233` takes the entity from a hidden bank field, so "which companies..." gets rejected for naming SUZLON, or answered for one stock (MB0928, MB0267). That also conflicts with the text-only rule in `QUERY_V4_DESIGN.md` §C.2. Run them as set queries over the pilot universe and state that scope in the answer, or widen as above.

**Evidence:** 489 of 1,073 parents are non-stock (measured). `rank` is used on 12 parts, `screen` 4, `join_entity_set` 4. Hand-check rows #31, #32, #35, #38 and #39 are all sets answered with one company.

**Risk:** a theme answer limited to 60 names is honest only if it says so, and it may be a weak answer. Option (a) looks like a re-scope, and that is his call. Widening costs more, and I have not sized the chunking.

**Cheap test:** run 20 set questions on the pilot universe by hand, then ask Shivam whether a scoped answer would satisfy him. If not, only widening remains.

## 4. What I would kill

- **The 30% strict checkpoint.** At this precision, 30% strict is about 2% truly correct. Replace it with a truth-panel target.
- **Decomposer recall and precision as progress measures**, until they score operator and window.
- **`--regress` on the old 61 as a quality signal.** 25 of the 40 lost are entity scope by construction, and the old "answered" set was itself unverified.
- **Further WP8 label gates, and WP7 band work for the gate.** `own.estimate` is 4 parts. WP9 and WP10 are already behind the gate, so keep them there.
