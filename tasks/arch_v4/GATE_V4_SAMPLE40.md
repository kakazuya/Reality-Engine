# v4 gate — precision hand-check of 40 strict-ANSWERED rows (2026-10-31)

How the 40 were drawn: `python tasks/pilot60/gate_run.py v4sample40` — a fixed seed
(`SEED40 = 20261030`), stratified round-robin over `bucket × family`, from the 211
strict-ANSWERED rows that are **not** in the hand-60 set (so no row here was ever
hand-read before). The drawn rows and the dump are regenerated by
`python tasks/pilot60/gate_run.py v4sample40` and `... v4dump40` (fixed seed
`SEED40 = 20261030`); both files are ~8 MB of generated JSON/text and are deliberately
not committed. Every row's full result and provenance stays readable in the gitignored
`tasks/answer_bench/GATE_RESULTS_v4_strict.jsonl` by `mid`.

Each row was judged against (a) the question as written, (b) the rendered answer, and
(c) the stored row(s) behind it — `atom`, `entity_id`, `period_end`, `unit`,
`known_at`, `source_ref` read back from `fact_v4_pilot.db`. Where the stored value
itself was wrong, that is said so.

Verdicts: **RIGHT** = the answer answers the question, with a verified value.
**PARTIAL** = the number/passage is real and on-topic, but only one reading of a
multi-part ask is served (the judgement or the comparison is missing).
**WRONG** = the value may be real, but the answer does not answer the question (a
different question is answered, or it is about another company).

## What this means (said plainly, not hidden)

**The 8.2% strict figure is not 8.2% correct.** Of 40 randomly drawn strict-ANSWERED
rows, 3 answer the question (7.5%); 8 carry the right number for one reading of the
ask (20.0%); 29 are contract-complete but answer a different question (72.5%).

Three distinct causes, each owned by a different piece:

1. **The decomposer reduces judgement asks to scalars** (#2, #6, #8, #9, #18, #24,
   #28, #40): "is the moat earning a premium" becomes `price.close` + `price.volume`.
   The grader certifies the contract, not the meaning. This is the largest single
   cause and it is a decomposer defect, not a store one.
2. **Instantiated cases answer the substituted company for the original question**
   (#3, #10, #13, #19): `instantiate.py` rewrote `entities` but not the text, so
   "Ola Electric after listing" is answered with Bosch/DDEVPLSTIK rows. The runner's
   case-entity resolution is right for the bank's definition of the instance; the
   instance itself is ill-posed. The fix is in `instantiate.py` (rewrite the text) or
   in the bank (mark the substituted predicate unanswerable).
3. **The store carries wrong or unusable rows** (#33/#34 wrong entity on a
   `list_distinct` ask; #35 a June shareholding row offered for a September ask):
   these are WP2a build defects and belong to the store owner. Row #4 is **not** in
   this class: its +34,552 cr matches the filing's own FY26 total comprehensive
   income (34,564 cr, `results_notes`, known 2026-08-10). It is a wrong *answer* to an
   accounting question, not a wrong row.

Only 3 of the 40 are right, so **a 30% strict target is not reachable by tuning the
contract checks**: the same run at 30% strict with this precision profile would still
be ~2.3% truly correct. The honest next step is (a) stop grading a scalar as an answer
when the ask is a judgement, and (b) fix the three pieces above, before raising volume.
